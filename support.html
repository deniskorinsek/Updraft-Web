Frequently asked questions

The flyer doesn’t react to my breath

Start with Settings → Recalibrate breath (about 15 seconds) in a quiet room — the game learns your phone’s microphone and your normal breath. Then:

  • Hold the phone 10–20 cm from your mouth and blow steadily rather than hard. A long, even breath lifts more than a short, loud one.
  • Check that nothing covers the microphone — a thick case, a finger, a pop socket.
  • Make sure UPDRAFT has the microphone permission in Android settings, and that no other app is using the microphone.
  • Raise Breath sensitivity in Settings if your phone’s microphone is quiet.
The flyer lifts when I talk or when it’s noisy

Recalibrate in the place where you usually play, so the game learns the background noise, and lower Breath sensitivity a notch. Breath has a very different sound from speech, but a loud room can still fool the detector.

Can I play without the microphone?

Yes. Touch Mode plays the whole game by touch: hold anywhere in the lower part of the screen to lift, tap for a Gust, and steer by tilting or dragging. Switch between runs in Settings or when the game offers it. Touch runs have their own leaderboards, so the comparison stays fair.

Does the game record me?

No. The microphone is only open while you fly, the sound is turned into a few numbers on your phone, and the audio is discarded immediately. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. The privacy policy has the details.

Tilt steering feels off or reversed
  • Hold the phone the way you like to play and choose Recenter tilt in the pause menu or in Settings. That position becomes neutral; it works upright, reclined or lying down.
  • Adjust Tilt sensitivity or switch on Invert tilt in Settings.
  • If your phone has no tilt sensor, the game offers swipe steering automatically: drag left or right anywhere to steer.
Why does the game ask for my age?

We ask once so that ads and privacy settings are set up correctly for younger players. Your answer stays on the device and is not sent to us.

I lost my progress after reinstalling or changing phones
  • Progress comes back through Android Auto Backup when backups are on for your Google account and the new phone restores from them.
  • Purchases are tied to your Google account: open the shop and tap Restore purchases.
  • Achievements come back when you sign in to Google Play Games.
I watched an ad but didn’t get the reward

A reward is granted when the ad network confirms you watched the whole ad; closing early gives nothing. If the connection dropped or the app was interrupted, the reward is granted the next time the game can confirm it. Still missing? Write to us with the time, your device and what you were doing, and we will sort it out.

A purchase didn’t arrive

Some payment methods take a while to clear; the item shows as pending until Google confirms it. After that, Restore purchases in the shop brings it in. If it is still missing after a day, send us the Google Play order number.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Privacy → Delete my data removes your progress and scores from the phone and creates a new install ID. Purchases stay restorable. For Google Play Games data use the Play Games app; for anything else, e-mail us.

Which phones are supported?

Android 10 or newer with a microphone. Tilt steering needs a rotation or gravity sensor; phones without one steer by swiping. Tablets and foldables are supported in portrait.

Found a bug?

Use Settings → Help & feedback in the game, or e-mail support@updraft.si. Please include your phone model, the Android version, what you did and what happened. A screenshot or a short screen recording helps a lot. We read everything — no account needed.

Pogosta vprašanja

Letalec se ne odziva na moj dih

Začni z Nastavitve → Ponovno umeri dih (približno 15 sekund) v tihem prostoru – igra se nauči mikrofona tvojega telefona in tvojega običajnega diha. Nato:

  • Telefon drži 10–20 cm od ust in pihaj enakomerno, ne močno. Dolg, enakomeren dih dvigne več kot kratek in glasen.
  • Preveri, da mikrofona nič ne prekriva – debel ovitek, prst, držalo.
  • Prepričaj se, da ima UPDRAFT v nastavitvah Androida dovoljenje za mikrofon in da mikrofona ne uporablja druga aplikacija.
  • Če je mikrofon tvojega telefona tih, v nastavitvah zvišaj občutljivost diha.
Letalec se dvigne, ko govorim ali ko je hrupno

Ponovno umeri dih tam, kjer običajno igraš, da se igra nauči hrupa v ozadju, in za stopnjo znižaj občutljivost diha. Dih zveni zelo drugače kot govor, a glasen prostor lahko detektor vseeno zmede.

Lahko igram brez mikrofona?

Da. Dotikalni način omogoča igranje celotne igre z dotikom: za vzgon pridrži kjer koli v spodnjem delu zaslona, tapni za sunek, krmari z nagibanjem ali vlečenjem. Preklopiš med vožnjami v nastavitvah ali ko ti igra to ponudi. Dotikalne vožnje imajo svoje lestvice, da ostane primerjava poštena.

Me igra snema?

Ne. Mikrofon je odprt samo med letom, zvok se na telefonu pretvori v nekaj številk, posnetek pa se takoj zavrže. Nič se ne shranjuje ali pošilja. Podrobnosti so v politiki zasebnosti.

Krmarjenje z nagibom je čudno ali obrnjeno
  • Telefon drži tako, kot rad igraš, in izberi Ponastavi nagib v meniju premora ali v nastavitvah. Ta položaj postane nevtralen; deluje pokonci, naslonjen ali leže.
  • V nastavitvah prilagodi občutljivost nagiba ali vklopi Obrni nagib.
  • Če telefon nima senzorja nagiba, igra samodejno ponudi krmarjenje z drsanjem: za krmarjenje povleci levo ali desno kjer koli.
Zakaj me igra vpraša za starost?

Vprašamo enkrat, da so oglasi in nastavitve zasebnosti pravilno nastavljeni za mlajše igralce. Tvoj odgovor ostane na napravi in se nam ne pošilja.

Po ponovni namestitvi ali menjavi telefona sem izgubil napredek
  • Napredek se vrne prek Android Auto Backup, če so varnostne kopije za tvoj Googlov račun vklopljene in se nov telefon obnovi iz njih.
  • Nakupi so vezani na tvoj Googlov račun: odpri trgovino in tapni Obnovi nakupe.
  • Dosežki se vrnejo, ko se prijaviš v Google Play Games.
Gledal sem oglas, nagrade pa nisem dobil

Nagrada se podeli, ko oglasno omrežje potrdi, da si si ogledal cel oglas; predčasno zapiranje ne da ničesar. Če je povezava padla ali je bila aplikacija prekinjena, se nagrada podeli naslednjič, ko jo igra lahko potrdi. Še vedno manjka? Piši nam s časom, napravo in tem, kaj si počel, pa bomo uredili.

Nakup ni prispel

Nekateri načini plačila potrebujejo nekaj časa; predmet je prikazan kot čakajoč, dokler ga Google ne potrdi. Potem ga Obnovi nakupe v trgovini prinese v igro. Če ga po enem dnevu še vedno ni, nam pošlji številko naročila Google Play.

Kako izbrišem svoje podatke?

Nastavitve → Zasebnost → Izbriši moje podatke odstrani tvoj napredek in rezultate s telefona ter ustvari nov ID namestitve. Nakupi ostanejo obnovljivi. Za podatke Google Play Games uporabi aplikacijo Play Games; za vse ostalo nam piši.

Kateri telefoni so podprti?

Android 10 ali novejši z mikrofonom. Krmarjenje z nagibom potrebuje senzor vrtenja ali težnosti; telefoni brez njega krmarijo z drsanjem. Tablice in zložljivi telefoni so podprti v pokončnem načinu.

Si našel napako?

Uporabi Nastavitve → Pomoč in povratne informacije v igri ali piši na support@updraft.si. Prosimo, navedi model telefona, različico Androida, kaj si naredil in kaj se je zgodilo. Posnetek zaslona ali kratek videoposnetek zelo pomaga. Vse preberemo – račun ni potreben.
