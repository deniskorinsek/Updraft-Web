Privacy policy

How the UPDRAFT game and this website handle your data — the short version and the full one.

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Applies to the UPDRAFT Android app (si.updraft) and this website

The short version

  • Your breath stays on your phone. The microphone is analysed on the device, in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored, logged or sent anywhere.
  • No account is needed to play. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not build advertising profiles ourselves.
  • Analytics are game events only (which screens you use, how runs go), tied to a random install ID, never to your name or e-mail.
  • Ads are optional. There are no banners and no interstitials; a rewarded ad only plays when you choose it.
  • You can delete your data from the game at any time: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data.

Microphone

UPDRAFT is controlled by blowing into the phone, so the app asks for the RECORD_AUDIO permission. Here is exactly what happens with it:

  • The microphone is open only while you fly: during breath calibration, the tutorial, the moment before a run starts, the countdown, the run itself and the short resume after a pause. It is closed in menus, during ads and whenever the app is in the background.
  • The sound is turned into a few numbers on the device, many times per second: how strong the blow is, how confident the detector is, whether a Gust was detected and how good the input quality is. Only these numbers are used; the audio itself is discarded immediately.
  • No audio is ever written to storage, to logs, to analytics or to crash reports, and nothing is sent to a server. We never ask players for audio samples.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings. The game remains playable without it in Touch Mode (lift by holding the screen).

Motion sensors

Steering uses the phone’s rotation or gravity sensors to measure how far you tilt it. The angle is processed on the device while you play and is not stored or transmitted. If your phone has no suitable sensor, you steer by swiping instead.

Ads & consent

  • UPDRAFT shows rewarded ads only — to revive a run, to double a reward or to boost the daily chest. Every one of them is your choice, and there is a daily cap. There are no banners and no forced ads between runs.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent before any personalised advertising, using Google’s consent framework. The prompt appears after your first results screen, never before your first flight. You can change your choices any time under Settings → Privacy options.
  • UPDRAFT is made for a general, family-friendly audience and is not directed at children. If you tell the game that you are under the age of digital consent in your country, ads are configured as non-personalised.

Purchases

Cosmetic items and the starter pack are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and shares only the order details we need to deliver the purchase. Purchases are restored automatically on the same Google account, or with Settings → Restore purchases. Nothing in the shop affects physics or fairness — everything is cosmetic.

Your data on your device & backups

  • Your progress — records, unlocks, Wind Dust, settings, calibration — is stored on your phone.
  • Android may back it up with Android Auto Backup to your Google Drive, encrypted by Google, so that it comes back after a reinstall. Breath calibration and the install ID are excluded because they are specific to one device. You can turn backups off in Android settings.
  • Settings → Privacy → Delete my data erases your progress and scores on the device and creates a new install ID. Purchases stay restorable through Google Play. Data held by Google Play Games can be removed in Google’s Play Games settings.

Children

UPDRAFT has no chat, no messaging and no user-generated content; the only social feature is a share card you create yourself. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export the data we hold, to restrict or object to its processing, and you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Because the game does not know who you are, we may ask for your install ID (Settings → About) to find your data. You can also complain to your supervisory authority; in Slovenia that is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec).

We keep data only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it.

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. We add no cookies, no analytics and no tracking of our own.
  • Your language choice is kept in your browser’s local storage so the site opens in the same language next time.
  • The breath demo on the home page can use your microphone only if you tap the microphone button and allow it. The sound is analysed inside your browser and is never recorded or uploaded. It stops when you leave the demo.

Changes to this policy

When we change how the game handles data, we update this page and the date at the top, and the game shows the new version on its Privacy screen.

Mikrofon

UPDRAFT krmiliš s pihanjem v telefon, zato aplikacija zahteva dovoljenje RECORD_AUDIO. Točno tako ga uporabljamo:

  • Mikrofon je odprt samo med letom: med umerjanjem diha, v vadnici, tik pred začetkom vožnje, med odštevanjem, med vožnjo in med kratkim nadaljevanjem po premoru. Zaprt je v menijih, med oglasi in vedno, ko je aplikacija v ozadju.
  • Zvok se na napravi večkrat na sekundo pretvori v nekaj številk: kako močan je pih, kako prepričan je detektor, ali je bil zaznan sunek in kakšna je kakovost vnosa. Uporabljajo se samo te številke; zvok sam se takoj zavrže.
  • Zvok se nikoli ne zapiše v shrambo, dnevnike, analitiko ali poročila o napakah in nič se ne pošilja na strežnik. Igralcev nikoli ne prosimo za zvočne vzorce.
  • Dovoljenje lahko kadar koli prekličeš v nastavitvah Androida. Igra ostane igralna tudi brez njega, v dotikalnem načinu (vzgon s pridržanjem zaslona).

Senzorji gibanja

Krmarjenje uporablja senzorje vrtenja ali težnosti telefona, da izmeri, kako močno ga nagneš. Kot se obdeluje na napravi med igranjem in se ne shranjuje ali pošilja. Če telefon nima ustreznega senzorja, krmariš z drsanjem.

Oglasi in privolitev

  • UPDRAFT prikazuje samo nagradne oglase – za oživitev vožnje, podvojitev nagrade ali okrepitev dnevne skrinje. Vsak je tvoja izbira in dnevno je omejen. Pasic in prisilnih oglasov med vožnjami ni.
  • V Evropskem gospodarskem prostoru, Združenem kraljestvu in Švici te pred kakršnim koli prilagojenim oglaševanjem prosimo za privolitev z Googlovim okvirom za privolitev. Poziv se prikaže po tvojem prvem zaslonu z rezultati, nikoli pred prvim letom. Izbire lahko kadar koli spremeniš v Nastavitve → Možnosti zasebnosti.
  • UPDRAFT je narejen za splošno, družinam prijazno občinstvo in ni namenjen otrokom. Če igri poveš, da si mlajši od starosti digitalne privolitve v svoji državi, so oglasi nastavljeni kot neprilagojeni.

Nakupi

Kozmetični predmeti in začetni paket se prodajajo prek Google Play Billing. Google obdela plačilo in nam posreduje samo podatke o naročilu, ki jih potrebujemo za dostavo nakupa. Nakupi se samodejno obnovijo na istem Googlovem računu ali z Nastavitve → Obnovi nakupe. Nič v trgovini ne vpliva na fiziko ali poštenost – vse je kozmetično.

Tvoji podatki na napravi in varnostne kopije

  • Tvoj napredek – rekordi, odklepi, vetrni prah, nastavitve, umerjanje – je shranjen na tvojem telefonu.
  • Android ga lahko z Android Auto Backup varnostno kopira v tvoj Google Drive, šifrirano pri Googlu, da se po ponovni namestitvi vrne. Umerjanje diha in ID namestitve sta izvzeta, ker sta vezana na eno napravo. Varnostne kopije lahko izklopiš v nastavitvah Androida.
  • Nastavitve → Zasebnost → Izbriši moje podatke izbriše tvoj napredek in rezultate na napravi ter ustvari nov ID namestitve. Nakupi ostanejo obnovljivi prek Google Play. Podatke pri Google Play Games odstraniš v Googlovih nastavitvah Play Games.

Otroci

UPDRAFT nima klepeta, sporočanja in uporabniških vsebin; edina družabna funkcija je kartica za deljenje, ki jo ustvariš sam. Osebnih podatkov otrok zavestno ne zbiramo. Če meniš, da nam je otrok posredoval osebne podatke, nam piši in jih bomo izbrisali.

Tvoje pravice

Po GDPR lahko zahtevaš dostop do podatkov, ki jih hranimo, njihov popravek, izbris ali izvoz, omejitev ali ugovor obdelavi, privolitev pa lahko kadar koli prekličeš, ne da bi to vplivalo na prejšnjo obdelavo. Ker igra ne ve, kdo si, te bomo morda prosili za ID namestitve (Nastavitve → O igri), da najdemo tvoje podatke. Pritožiš se lahko tudi pri nadzornem organu; v Sloveniji je to Informacijski pooblaščenec.

Podatke hranimo le, dokler so potrebni za zgornje namene, nato jih izbrišemo ali anonimiziramo.
