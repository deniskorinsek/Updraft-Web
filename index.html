How it works

Easy to learn. Hard to master.

Three things to learn and a whole sky to climb. The difficulty comes from wind, timing and space — never from random unfairness.

  1. 1
    Your breath is the engine

    Blow to fly

    A gentle puff gives a little lift. A long, steady blow becomes a Gust that carries you fast. Time it right for a Perfect.

  2. 2
    Your hands set the course

    Tilt to steer

    Lean your phone left or right to drift through gaps, slip past branches and line up the next wind ring.

  3. 3
    The sky does the rest

    Ride the wind

    Updrafts and thermals lift you for free; crosswinds push you off course. Read the air, chain near misses into combos and watch your Wind Energy grow.

Prefer not to blow? Touch Mode lets you play the whole game with a tap.

Three ways to fly

Same breath. Completely different physics.

Every flyer has its own weight, its own world and its own way of failing. Learn one, then fall for the next.

Feather

The Natural

Elegant, responsive and forgiving. The Feather rewards precise steering and well-timed Gusts — the flyer for chasing records through the Meadow, the Forest and up past the Sunset.

  • Balanced lift
  • Good steering
  • Perfect Gusts

Bubble

The Dreamer

Ultra light and sensitive to every breath. Three touches and it pops, so the Bubble is about planning ahead and threading the narrow gaps between the floating isles.

  • High lift
  • Very drifty
  • Three lives

Snowflake

The Wind Dancer

Don’t force it through the world — dance with the wind. The Snowflake falls slowly, spirals in crosswinds and loves a good current, all the way from the Snowy Forest to the Aurora Sky.

  • Slow fall
  • Spiral drift
  • Rides the currents

Skins and trails are cosmetic only — same physics for everyone.
